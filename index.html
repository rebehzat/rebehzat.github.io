02 / About

Curiosity before credentials

I’m Recai, a developer and Linux enthusiast following the IB Diploma Programme. My higher-level subjects are Mathematics AA, Physics, and English. Outside school I spend most of my time around backend development, infrastructure, networking, security, electronics, and open-source software.

My long-term direction is DevSecOps. I’m still learning—especially in penetration testing and security research—and I’d rather show the work honestly than pretend to be a senior engineer.

I like systems that can be opened, modified, rooted, reprogrammed, self-hosted, repaired, and understood. An old phone or laptop with an unlocked bootloader is usually more interesting to me than a polished device designed to stay closed.
