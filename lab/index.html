VERIFIED NOTES

Linux & infrastructure

I use Linux daily and have worked across Ubuntu, Fedora, and Arch. My infrastructure toolkit includes Docker, Docker Compose, Git, SSH, networking, CI/CD, and self-hosted homelab services.

Local AI

I use Ollama and local LLMs extensively, with an interest in agents, MCP, RAG, orchestration, and developer tooling. Lumode is the clearest public example: a local coding-agent shell with sessions, tool calls, context management, and streaming output.

Electronics

I work with Raspberry Pi, ESP32, ESP8266, and Arduino-compatible boards. WiFiGotchi combines an ESP8266 with a 1602 display; the public repository includes its pin layout and firmware.

Reverse engineering

The Shannon S310 project documents a verified replacement for Samsung’s proprietary cbd modem loader. The replacement owns the boot-control device while Samsung RIL retains IPC/RFS, and it was tested through calls, SMS, LTE, reboot persistence, and rollback.
